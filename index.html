About Kampuram Business Group

Our story is rooted in Manjeri, Kerala, and in a founder’s close connection to writing and literature. Today, Kampuram Business Group brings together advertising, digital design, wedding invitations, and books.

Each business has its own focus: an advertisement for a local business, an invitation for a wedding, a video for social media, or a book to spend time with. This website brings them together so you can browse and speak to us about what you need.

Our aim is to help people and businesses communicate through thoughtful words, design and print.
